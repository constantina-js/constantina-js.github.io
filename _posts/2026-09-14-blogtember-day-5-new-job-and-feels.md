I haven't even started writing the post for today and I can already feel how weird it's going to be. I don't know how to start this. I also don't even know how to feel about putting this facet of my life out there like that.

I guess we can start with the good thing.

I started a new job today as a cash admin at a big store in the city. It is a full-time role with all the benefits I want/need right now, and it's in an area of operations that is pretty new to me. I dubbed it in my mind as a "skillsmaxxing" job with a lot of whimsy in it. I also love and appreciate how my leadership team is very much chill in the sense that they acknowledge that our job isn't *that* serious and that everything is fixable/very, very few things are detrimental. Which is very reassuring. 

On the other side of things...

For context: I am an astrology girlie and regularly read and listen to my astrology readings on the Chani app. Today, my reading said that there would be a relationship pattern/dynamic/issue that will begin to surface. 

There is someone I've been talking to for a few months, and we had developed a mutual liking for one another. Something had been triggering my anxiety with regard to where we stood, and after going back and forth about bringing it up, I decided to rip the Band-Aid off. The last thing I wanted to do was build potential resentment. Plus, we've been really good about communicating in general; it's been the most communicative romantic-adjacent relationship I've ever had. 

And while we did have a very honest conversation, I can tell things are about to end and I am grieving that. I am grieving the end of a beautiful chapter with someone who I have come to really care for. I have no regrets about starting that conversation. I know in the past I would not have done that, and would just rot in my own anxiety. So for that, I am proud of myself. 

But it doesn't take away this sadness. 

And who knows...maybe this isn't the end. Maybe it is *just* an issue. But...I don't know. I'm mad that the reading for today was pretty on point and I have a bone to pick with all the planets in Aries and Venus in Scorpio/Venus about to go into retrograde.
