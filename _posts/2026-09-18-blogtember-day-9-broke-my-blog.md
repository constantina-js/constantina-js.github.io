So shortly after I published yesterday's post, I noticed a big, glaring issue on my blog's UI. 

Actually, make that two issues because I found another bug.

The first bug I found had to do with the post list that gets generated when you land on the blog section of my personal website. Instead of showing a list of posts in chronological order from newest to oldest and then paginating that list, it only shows the first blog post, along with links to navigate to a previous page and to another post. Both of those links send you to an incomplete URL.

I caught today's issue when I clicked the link to the first post and tried to navigate to another post from there. It turns out there is a separate issue with the navigation between individual posts: instead of taking you to the next or previous post, the link leads to an incomplete URL.

I honestly completely forgot to check it out after work today, and though I have been relaxing this entire evening by playing Fear & Hunger and Tekken 8 (the latter of which was a very productive grinding session), I would rather table fixing this issue to enjoy my Friday evening and try to fix this tomorrow when I have the whole day to myself.
