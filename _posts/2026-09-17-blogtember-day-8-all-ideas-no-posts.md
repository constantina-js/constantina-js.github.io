One of the most frustrating things about this challenge, and trying to keep a blog in general, is having too many ideas about what to write come at inconvenient times. Usually, these ideas come at a time when I am nowhere near my computer, and I am in the middle of something and I can't write anything beyond a short note to refer back to.

Then, when it comes time to actually write and expand the idea...I got nothing. Suddenly, the narration that was going on in my head goes dead silent. Even after I jot down the points I want to hit, the words just don't come out.

...or I get distracted by another idea. And then I open a new text file. And then I start bullet pointing. And then I get pulled into something else.

I'm hoping this weekend I can write something that I have been meaning to finally put into a text file. It's an idea that's been floating in my head for a while.
